Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Welcome to the developer website of Rakesh Kumar prusty ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled securely. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you visit our website or procure our software services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you contact us, request a quote, or sign service contracts. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, business name, email address, physical address, and phone number.
  • Project Information: Technical requirements, project scopes, database specifications, and source files.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the details we collect for the following purposes:

  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including financial audits and payment verification processes.

3. Sharing and Disclosing Information

We value your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share details with trusted partners who assist in operating our website, managing payment processing (e.g., payment gateways), or delivering services (only to the extent necessary).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government authority to comply with legal processes or protect our rights and safety.

4. Data Security

We employ standard technical and administrative measures to protect your details from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website is served over secure HTTPS (SSL), and any financial interactions are securely managed via certified payment processing channels.
